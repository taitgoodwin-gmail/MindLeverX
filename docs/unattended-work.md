## Limits, recovery and reporting

Each two-hour opportunity selects one unit, normally at most 30 minutes including review. Stop/checkpoint at the limit. Keep 20% of applicable reported account windows reserved; skip substantive work at 80% used or above. Unavailable usage means status/access-only work. These are prompt-level controls, not a hard metered budget or runtime guarantee. No new spending, resets or credit purchases are authorized.

One justified transient retry per run. Persist unchanged blockers and avoid retrying without new evidence. Move to another authorized ready task; return quietly if none exists.
