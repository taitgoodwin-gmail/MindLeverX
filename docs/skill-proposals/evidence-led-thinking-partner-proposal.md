Version 1.0, 17 September 2026. Supersedes review drafts v0.1/v0.2. The owner requested harmonization, conflict resolution, pressure testing and finalization. ## What is finalized

- Concise standing guidance in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md).
- Seven canonical [tenets](../product-tenets.md), including consumer psychology for buyers and I-O psychology for human work.
- Current qualitative [roadmap assessment](../mvp-tenet-assessment.md), with dependency priorities and historical numerical scores separated.
- [Metric definitions](../tenet-metrics.md), [methodology and provenance](../metric-methodology.md), and the reusable skill with conditional measurement/behavior references.

Use brief advice for ordinary decisions and fuller evidence review for consequential choices. Consider all seven principles; record material tradeoffs and only the relevant metrics. Psychology interventions and quantitative targets remain hypotheses/proposals, not validated effects or adopted SLAs. Finalizing the method does not approve a service price, provider purchase, redesign or overnight backlog expansion.

## Pressure-test corrections

An independent read-only specialist reviewed the seven-tenet framework. The coordinator accepted and corrected: six-versus-seven inconsistency; misleading leadership-claim threshold; 80% reuse as an implied gate; contradictory skill status; mechanical UX penalty for required approval; and excessive metric/reporting burden. Historical authority dates were clarified. Follow-up review caught one behavioral-metric denominator mismatch; both definitions now use all eligible people offered the action, with missing observations disclosed. Current priority is dependency readiness rather than a weighted score rewarding recurring delivery simply because recurrence is a goal.

## Validation and limits

The standard skill-creator validator passed after PyYAML 6.0.2 was installed in an isolated temporary validation environment. No project or global runtime dependency was added. This checks packaging, not behavior.

A separate agent received the skill and five raw offline user requests, without the proposed acceptance table or intended answers. It read both conditional references. Actual responses and coordinator review are retained in [the trial record](../thinking-partner-trial.md). The trial tested claim caution, automation versus human effort, behavioral diagnosis, a simple heading edit and portal prioritization. A claim-presentation concern prompted one targeted revision and follow-up test; see that record for its outcome.

These are bounded AI-assisted trials, not representative customer tests, proof of enduring reliability, a benchmark of the model or automatic skill discovery. The agent explicitly read the skill; fresh-session implicit discovery remains unverified. On18September, the available-skills catalog included the skill and this task received the project instructions. That confirms presence/discovery; reliable implicit selection and future adherence remain unverified. No continuously running specialist team is implied.
