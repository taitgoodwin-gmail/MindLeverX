# Seven-tenet contribution profiles

These are qualitative review judgments for the proposed behavior, not measurements or benchmarks. Direct contribution does not mean implemented. Every requirement names a profile and separately records owner/client friction and material authority conflicts. Unknown means evidence is missing; not relevant means the decision does not materially affect that tenet. No weighting or composite score.
