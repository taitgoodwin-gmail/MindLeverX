# MindLeverX: market evidence to prioritized requirements

**Product-management review · 8 October 2026, America/New_York · status: proposed for owner review**

## Decision

Advance a trustworthy first paid result before broadening the dashboard. **P0** is to qualify what MindLeverX may collect and report, with honest unavailable states. **P1** is to prove that a client can understand and act on the first real report while the owner can review and release the exact version with little avoidable effort. A small public-site correction can run as a separate P1 communication slice after its current release source is located. **P2** defines the comparable recurring report that would give customers a reason to renew, then optional first-party measurement context. None of these ranks asserts implementation, customer acceptance or a measured commercial advantage.

The project's own `docs/product-tenets.md` defines P0 as the next blocking prerequisite, P1 as necessary for first paid delivery and P2 as repeated value and renewal. The [IIBA prioritization task card](https://www.iiba.org/contentassets/786c0fca473a4540b8bd2c494da43781/task-card-prioritize-req-and-designs.pdf) supports considering value, urgency, dependencies and risk. It does not prescribe these project ranks or an OpenAI requirements format. I used the Build project's existing evidence-led thinking-partner skill and three bounded AI reviews for product, UX/engineering and source governance; none is independent customer validation.

### 1. P0 — qualification and truthful states

**Current local wording.** `PROP-DATA-001 — Collection rights and evidence portability`: “Before accepting a collection route, verify the exact service and permitted collection, commercial reporting, retention, export, sensitive-data and cost boundaries; an unmet mandatory right or evidence field blocks acceptance or requires an explicitly narrowed offer.” `MLX3-MEA-012 — Distinct Measurement States`: “The data model and presentation distinguish not measured, failed, insufficient, observed absence and measured zero without coercing missing evidence to zero.” Both are review proposals, not accepted product scope.

**Proposed clarification to PROP-DATA-001:** “For each offered measurement surface, the release record identifies the qualified collection method and permitted commercial reporting, retention and export conditions. A surface missing a mandatory right or evidence field is unavailable or explicitly excluded with a reason; its absence is never sold or displayed as observed zero visibility.” This strengthens an existing proposal; it does not choose a provider or make a legal determination. The September Otterly support response is not proof of consumer-ChatGPT collection authority, and current API entitlement is unverified in the project record.

**Acceptance case PM-01 — NOT RUN.** Prerequisites: fixed permitted saved-evidence fixture, an intentionally unqualified second surface, frozen offer/method version and no live provider call. (1) Prepare the result. (2) Inspect the report and release checklist. **PASS** if the permitted observation cites its source/version, the second surface is unavailable or excluded with reason, and no zero or visibility claim is inferred from it. **FAIL** if missing authority/evidence becomes a measured zero or an offered claim. **BLOCKED** if rights or the fixture cannot be established. Retain safe receipts; remove temporary private data. The first-sale gate remains MIN-5/MIN-12.

### 2. P1 — report and owner/client UX

**Current local wording.** `MLX3-RES-001`: “The client report opens with an understandable principal finding and its material limitation, with supporting method and evidence reachable through clearly labeled detail.” `MLX3-RES-007`: “The report identifies one prioritized evidence-linked action, or explains why none is justified, and discloses confidence, expected effort, dependencies and reversibility; recurring reports also state the known status of the prior action.” `PROP-UX-001`: “For a representative pilot, record client completion and comprehension and owner preparation, review and recovery effort separately, using the same defined journey and recording assistance and failures.” The first two are review drafts; the UX row is a proposal.

**Proposed acceptance addition, no new feature ID:** The initial view should let a representative reader identify the finding, material limit and justified action or no-action without coaching. The owner should find exceptions, inspect evidence and release or return the exact report version without rereading every raw answer. Record comprehension, assistance, time, errors and recovery separately for owner and client. Do not invent a numeric target from a synthetic test.

**Acceptance case PM-02 — NOT RUN.** Prerequisites: pinned build/browser/assistive-technology versions and one synthetic report with four eligible observations, two mentions, one refused attempt, one excluded anchored attempt, a small-sample limitation and one evidence-linked action. (1) Open desktop and narrow mobile layouts. (2) Traverse finding, limitation, evidence and action by keyboard and screen reader. (3) Ask a representative reader, without coaching, to explain those three elements; record their answer and assistance. (4) Have the owner review exceptions and act on that exact draft version; record effort and outcome. **PASS** for correct values/state/denominator adjacent to claims, working evidence path and applicable tested WCAG criteria; record human comprehension and effort as observations. **FAIL** for hidden limitation, unsupported claim, broken focus/status/reflow or incorrect release identity. **BLOCKED** for the human portion without a representative participant or reviewable build. Delete synthetic/private working data and retain deidentified observations. Targeted checks do not establish full [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/) conformance.

### 3. P1 site communication and learning loop

**Candidate wording, ID unassigned pending owner scope decision:** “For a material public GEO/AI insight change, record the buyer question, dated primary evidence, original MindLeverX analysis, affected requirement or limitation, exact copy and UI diff, approved release version, intended visitor action and follow-up observation. Verify factual claims and accessibility before release. Do not treat impressions, a Lighthouse score or coincident analytics movement as proof that content caused revenue.” This is an **internal operating requirement**, not a promise that the product already collects the data.

The live [GEO explainer](https://mindleverx.com/what-is-geo.html), read 8 October, emphasizes citations/mentions/share of voice while Google's [Search Console AI report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139) also exposes AI-feature link impressions. The exact source branch for the live page differs from this checkout. Proposed small edit: explain Search Console impressions, separately identifiable visits/actions in analytics, and sampled answer mentions/citations with their individual limits. Do not replace the owner-selected design or add an unsupported composite score. Google's [AI Search guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) keeps ordinary SEO in scope; its [AI-content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content) requires accuracy and usefulness across generated page content and metadata.

**Acceptance case PM-03 — NOT RUN.** Prerequisites: verified release source and public page version, approved copy diff, property access only if actual measurement is attempted. (1) Compare live page with exact release source. (2) Preview proposed copy and design at desktop/mobile, keyboard and assistive-technology paths; inspect source claims and links. (3) Run Lighthouse as a diagnostic and check applicable manual WCAG criteria; keep real-user Core Web Vitals separate. (4) After authorized release, verify hosted bytes/copy and observe the intended visitor action using an already authorized GA4 event if configured. **PASS** for source-backed copy, preserved journey, relevant accessibility checks, exact release receipt and honest measurement state. **FAIL** for unsupported capability/causal claims or broken flow. **BLOCKED** for production comparison or outcome attribution when release source, analytics access or consent is unavailable. Lighthouse is a lab audit, not accessibility conformance or real-user experience proof ([Chrome Lighthouse](https://developer.chrome.com/docs/lighthouse/overview); [W3C evaluation guidance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/test-evaluate/tools/selecting/)).

### 4. P2 — recurring comparison as the Otterly response

Otterly's own [5 October changelog](https://help.otterly.ai/changelog) describes sentiment, position, annotations and trend views in Brand Reports. These are vendor claims, not a verified buyer advantage. The specific MindLeverX opportunity is to prevent misleading change stories when the tracked question panel grows. Existing local `MLX3-MEA-005/011/014/015` and `MLX3-SVC-006` already require frozen versions, denominator context, visible boundaries and no unsupported movement or causal claims. **No duplicate “copy Otterly” requirement is recommended.**

**Acceptance case PM-04 — NOT RUN.** Prerequisites: fixed synthetic cycle C1 with 20 mentions in 50 prompts; successor C2 has the same original 50 with 20 mentions and 50 new prompts with 10 mentions; pinned method/context and a dated “page published” annotation. (1) Render report, chart, table and export. (2) Inspect keyboard and text alternative. **PASS** if C1 is 20/50 = 40%, C2 is 30/100 = 30% with distinct panels, common cohort remains 20/50 = 40% where shown, the unbridged aggregate is not called a 10-point decline, and the annotation is not given causal credit. **FAIL** for a silent bridge, unsupported cause or inaccessible chart-only conclusion. **BLOCKED** for a positive change claim until the supported-change method is accepted. Clean synthetic fixtures after execution.

### 5. P2 — source-specific measurements

`MLX3-MEA-013` already separates technical access, answer readiness, off-site evidence, observed AI visibility, ordinary search and model-API benchmarks, and prohibits an unsupported overall composite. Extend its acceptance fixtures before changing the wording: a Google AI-feature impression is not an exact prompt or answer citation; sampled Bing citations are not visits; GA4's AI Assistant channel does not include Google AI Overviews/AI Mode traffic classified as Organic Search. Show source, date, population, known limits and not-measured state for each. Connectors remain optional until authorization and buyer value are demonstrated. Sources checked 8 October: [Search Console AI report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139), [Bing AI Performance](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/ai-performance-9f8e7d6c), [GA4 channel definitions](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9756891).

**Current local wording.** `MLX3-MEA-013 — Separate Measurement Families`: “Reports keep website technical access, answer readiness, off-site evidence and observed AI visibility distinguishable, keep ordinary search performance and model-API benchmarks separate when present, and display no unsupported overall composite.” This is a review draft, not implemented source integration.

**Acceptance case PM-05 — NOT RUN.** Prerequisites: fixed synthetic exports with one Search Console AI-feature link impression, a separate sampled Bing citation, an identifiable GA4 AI Assistant referral/key event, a Google AI Overview visit classified as Organic Search, and one unavailable source; pinned source definitions and no live connector. (1) Import/display each source. (2) Inspect the report and export. **PASS** if each retains its source, date, population and limits; impressions, citations, referrals and actions stay separate; the unavailable source is not zero; no overall GEO score or causal lift appears. **FAIL** if populations are merged or a prompt/click/visit is invented. **BLOCKED** for live-property validation without authorized access. Remove the synthetic imports after the test.

## Seven-tenet tradeoff and priority challenge

The two near-term requirements directly protect **client and owner UX** through clear limits and review, and support **repeatability** and **automation** with fixed states and exact report versions. They may enable **recurring revenue**, but payment and renewal are unmeasured. **AI intelligence** is useful only if evidence-backed interpretation beats a simpler deterministic report at acceptable review cost (`PROP-AI-001` remains a proposal). **Competitive leadership** is a testable claim about buyer usefulness, traceability, effort and cost, not vendor feature count (`PROP-LEAD-001`). **Behavioral design** here means helping a buyer understand uncertainty and take an informed next action; the mechanism and outcome need user testing.

The strongest objection is that five tracks could delay the first sale. Limit active work to the P0 qualification and P1 first-report review, with the narrow site correction handled only after exact source verification. Specify P2 tests now, but do not build new connectors or charts ahead of a qualified cycle. Ranks change if a qualified alternative collection method resolves P0, if representative readers show the current report already works, if a real buyer requires multi-source data, or if the accepted first offer promises time trends.
