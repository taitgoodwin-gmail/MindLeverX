# Requirements review — changes and decisions

Prepared 18 September 2026 Eastern. This is the short reading path for the owner. The [revised register](revised-requirements.md) contains the full wording and [source crosswalk](source-crosswalk.md) accounts for every original ID.

## Recommendation

Use this review copy to prepare the next evidence-to-report slice. Keep the original IDs, qualitative seven-tenet assessments, explicit owner/client friction and linked test cases. Do not require every feature to score positively on every tenet. An authorization boundary may create useful, necessary friction; deterministic automation does not become AI intelligence because a model helped write it.

The simpler credible alternative is to apply this format only to the next measurement/report slice and leave later production cases as clearly identified plans. That gets a real report sooner and avoids building the historical full admin portal before its value is demonstrated. The complete draft is still useful as a coverage map. Evidence of actual operational pain, pilot workload or customer access needs would justify bringing more of that portal into the first release.

## Material changes

1. **Business and engine assumptions are no longer disguised as settled requirements.** Historical B2B SaaS-only, no-checkout, Perplexity-only and fixed panel-size claims are reconciled with September decisions. Buyer, offer, qualified collection and exact sample design remain explicit.
2. **Recurring automation stops at owner release.** A healthy report can still require an approval item. The old claim that a healthy month produces zero queue items conflicts with that rule and has been corrected.
3. **Missing evidence stays distinct from no change or zero.** Method boundaries and unavailable metadata are explicit. Vendor labels are not independent proof of consumer-platform equivalence.
4. **Reproducibility has two relevant contracts.** Deterministic measurements are recalculated; approved AI prose is retrieved from its stored version. No promise of identical new live AI answers is made.
5. **The current design direction remains authoritative.** Historical builder names and rejected Linear-inspired design do not govern this build. No appearance changes were made.
6. **Usability, economics and AI value have observable evaluation proposals.** All six existing live-Sheet `PROP-*` IDs are retained; five additional proposals cover whole-journey usability, portability, AI evaluation, competitive comparison and behavioral design. They remain proposals rather than retroactively approved features, numerical targets or achieved benefits.
7. **Verification is separated from status.** Existing local passing tests are bounded evidence. Proposed product cases are NOT RUN and name missing production prerequisites. A requirements rewrite cannot prove customer readiness.
