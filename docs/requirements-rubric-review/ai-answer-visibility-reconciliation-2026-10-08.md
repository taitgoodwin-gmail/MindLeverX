## Product decisions

1. **First paid MindLeverX output:** a separately purchasable, one-time, evidence-backed baseline report. Recurring enrollment and site changes are not prerequisites. This follows the user's selected first-offer emphasis and the existing T01 boundary.
2. **AugMind position at launch:** AI Answer Visibility is a Digital Marketing Performance & Revenue add-on/use case, not a proven standalone service. AugMind may offer Implement and Run under separately scoped agreements. Reconsider a standalone offer only if representative buyers outside the existing relationship actually buy the baseline and want a separate product identity.
3. **Release:** owner review and an exact-version release decision apply to both one-time and monthly reports. The product never substitutes a service-side outside reviewer or client signature for that release decision. The PM-selected one-time policy is now recorded in the canonical register; this choice does not resume implementation.
4. **Measurement:** keep `PROP-DATA-001` and `PROP-UX-001` exactly as owner-approved. No provider is chosen. A surface without qualified rights or retained evidence is unavailable or excluded, not zero. T37 already tests the synthetic boundary; real-route qualification remains blocked.
5. **First-offer exclusions:** do not promise lead/revenue attribution, site or crawler changes, maker corrections, a fixed monthly/quarterly cadence, a mandatory outside reviewer, or exact deck section counts in the first MindLeverX report. Report any allegedly inaccurate answer as an evidence-backed, disputed or unverified observation, never as a correction guaranteed by a maker.

- **T06 / D02:** test that an unreleased one-time report is private; reject wrong recipient, missing evidence and a stale decision after content changes. Require a new owner decision on the successor version. Keep actual send/release separate from synthetic checks.
- **T23 / export:** export a fixed frozen panel with exact permitted question text, comparators, method/run context and versions; verify authorized access, no restricted fields, no secrets and no cross-tenant content. A new live AI answer is not expected to repeat byte-for-byte.
- **T37:** retain its current synthetic qualification case. For an actual route, attach current service/plan terms and a permitted retained observation. A manual route and a tool route need separate qualification if both are offered; a side-by-side comparison is not required merely because two routes exist.

## Evidence and rationale

The deck's shared measurement/report ideas map to existing requirements; copying its entire Discover/Implement/Run service model would confuse a first paid product report with optional agency work. A versioned offer-to-report contract closes a concrete buyer-trust gap. Portable permitted inputs let the client inspect the method without implying rights to third-party fields or guaranteeing repeat live answers. Exact-version owner release is the smallest consistent one-time policy and preserves the project's human decision boundary. These are PM choices, not externally mandated requirements.
