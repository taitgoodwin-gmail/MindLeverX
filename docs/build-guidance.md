## Continuous improvement during active work — 19 September 2026

The owner explicitly asks for continual improvement on every turn, including questioning early research and decisions, improving business outcomes and implementation, and using AI intelligently to reduce unnecessary work. This strengthens the existing thinking-partner practice. It is an active-work habit, not a new unattended schedule, standing agent team or global setting.

Use this lightweight loop:

1. Before acting, check whether the next step still serves the intended outcome, whether its dependency is real, and whether existing evidence or a simpler approach avoids work. Consider the seven tenets proportionately; do not perform a full rubric review for every small action.
2. Make reversible, low-risk improvements within the authorized work without a separate permission cycle. Examples: remove unnecessary duplication, fix stale status text, simplify implementation while preserving behavior, reuse verified evidence, and focus verification on the changed behavior.
3. For changes to accepted product direction, material behavior/appearance, architecture with substantial migration cost, spending, privacy, customer promises or release boundaries, prepare a concrete recommendation and explain the benefit, tradeoff and smallest validation before the shared decision. Do not silently overturn an accepted decision because it is old. Existing explicit authorizations continue to apply; do not ask again unnecessarily.
4. Verify the change at a level appropriate to its consequence. At the chunk handoff, briefly report a material improvement and its observed result; label expected benefits as unmeasured when appropriate. If no worthwhile improvement is evident, continue delivery without manufacturing one or adding a ceremonial update.

Keep one authoritative home for each requirement/decision, with a readable summary and link on the active Linear card. Preserve necessary full requirement narratives and test instructions in the chunk plan, as the owner requested, but do not create additional parallel registers. Correct stale copies when encountered and identify which current record supersedes historical text. Open the active Linear card beside the conversation, per `docs/chunk-plan.md`.

Separate a product defect, a missing fact, an unresolved owner decision and an external dependency. State the exact deliverable each blocks. Revisit whether an old or draft requirement is necessary for the claim being made; propose a narrower useful outcome when justified. Do not infer that a provider-qualification gap blocks all saved-data demonstrations, and do not infer that a passing demonstration resolves qualification.
