01 · The shift

AI answers are changing how buyers discover companies.

AI answers add another place where buyers can encounter a brand. We propose reviewing mentions and citations alongside business outcomes, without treating either as a substitute for visits, qualified leads or sales.

Controlled research · KDD 2024
Test changes in context
The GEO paper tested content changes on 1,000 queries using GPT-3.5 and five retrieved sources. Effects varied by tactic and domain; these findings do not establish MindLeverX customer lift. Read the GEO paper, v3 →
Our proposed approach
Compare like with like
Keep the question panel and collection conditions visible. A result on one engine is not evidence of the same result elsewhere.
Our proposed approach
Show evidence and its limits
Distinguish observed answers from interpretations, and missing evidence from zero. Test recommendations before claiming that a change caused an outcome.
