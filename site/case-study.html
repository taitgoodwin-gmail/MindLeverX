01 · The panel

The panel is not locked yet — so it is not published yet.

Measured visibility is the dimension every buyer wants first, and it is the one that cannot be faked. It requires a prompt panel: a fixed set of category questions, locked at a baseline, re-run on a schedule so that a later reading is comparable with an earlier one. A panel that changes between runs makes every trend meaningless — which is why the panel is versioned and why extension sets never edit the locked one.

MindLeverX has not locked its own panel. No baseline run exists, so there is nothing to publish here and nothing to trend. When the panel is locked, this section will carry the panel itself — the prompts, the version, the collection window — because a benchmark whose inputs are secret is a claim rather than a measurement.

02 · The scorecard

Four dimensions. Two readings. No composite.

Methodology V1.0 scores technical access, content quotability, off-site authority and measured visibility, then takes their unweighted mean. Two of those four are properties of the artifact and can be read right now. The other two are properties of the world — how the wider web treats the brand, and what the engines actually answer — and neither was measured in the original 27 July 2026 review.

04 · Method

How these numbers were taken.

Deployment status was checked by DNS lookup of mindleverx.com on 27 Jul 2026; the result was no A record, which is why every other reading on this page is scoped to the build artifact rather than a live origin. The rendering check loaded each page twice in headless Chromium — once with JavaScript enabled and once disabled — and compared the text of the main content region; a page passes only if disabling scripts removes nothing. The remaining checks are direct inspection of the 6 HTML files and of robots.txt, llms.txt and sitemap.xml.

Dimension scores are deliberately absent. Methodology V1.0 defines the four dimensions and the unweighted-mean composite, but the scorer that converts checks into a 0–100 dimension score is not built. Assigning one by hand would be an invented number wearing a methodology's name. The methodology is defined here →
