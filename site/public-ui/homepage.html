METHOD & LIMITS

How this report was made

Questions: 10, agreed with you before collection: 4 discovery, 3 comparison, 3 business facts.

Collection: each question asked twice, on 2 and 3 September 2026, in one AI assistant’s consumer interface, US English.

Website: homepage and 4 service pages, fetched and read as served.

Alternatives: 2, named by you, counted within the same 20 answers.

Labels

MEASURED (TOOL): observed directly, for example by fetching a page.

MEASURED (LIVE): seen in a live AI answer, dated.

INFERRED: deduced from indirect signals such as search results. Likely, not confirmed.

NOT MEASURED: could not be observed. We name what would unlock it.

Limits

20 answers is a small sample, not a market-wide estimate.

AI answers change between sessions, dates and phrasings.

This report makes no claim that any change will cause a result.

The readiness review covers 5 pages, not a full technical audit.
