Answer Engine Index: Q3 2026.

This sample article demonstrates how a quarterly benchmark could present results. Every numerical finding and engine-specific interpretation below is illustrative; no research panel was run for this report.

By MINDLEVERX RESEARCHExample period · JUL 2026Reading · 12 MINMethodology · V1.0
THREE ILLUSTRATIVE FINDINGSSAMPLE

  1. Average citation rate rose to 11.4% (+1.2pt vs Q2) across 1,200 tracked prompts — engines are citing more, not less, as answer surfaces mature.

  2. Top-decile brands now take 61% of all citations (+4pt). Visibility is concentrating: the engines increasingly agree on who the answer is.

  3. Freshness beat authority on Perplexity for the first time — pages updated within 90 days out-cited older, higher-authority pages in head-to-head prompts.

01 · Headline results

Every engine moved. Three moved up.

The example assumes a panel of 1,200 prompts — 300 per engine, spanning definitional, comparative and recommendation intents. These are illustrative inputs, not a completed Q3 or Q2 run. Citation rate measures how often a tracked brand's own domain appears as a cited source inside the answer.

Perplexity's jump is the quarter's outlier, and it isn't random: its retrieval now visibly favors recently updated pages. In matched-pair prompts where an older authoritative page competed with a fresher mid-authority page, the fresher page won 58% of citations — a reversal from Q2.

02 · What changed inside the engines

Fewer sources, chosen harder.

ChatGPT tightened its source pool again: unique domains cited per answer fell from 3.1 to 2.6 quarter-over-quarter. Gemini leaned further into structured data — pages with complete schema out-cited prose-only equivalents nearly two to one. Claude remained the most attribution-hungry engine, quoting dated and sourced claims at the highest rate of the four.

The combined effect is a market where the middle is disappearing. Brands in the top decile of our panel gained four points of citation share this quarter; brands below the median lost ground on every engine except Perplexity, where a freshness push can still buy visibility that authority alone no longer can.

03 · What the top decile does

The cited brands run the same four plays.

Reading across the top-decile brands, the pattern is consistent — and it maps directly onto the five moves we rank by leverage. They own a definitional pillar the engines quote verbatim. They put dated statistics inside quotable passages, not beside them. They are corroborated off their own site — G2, Reddit, Wikipedia, category roundups. And they monitor all four engines, because a Gemini schema fix does nothing for a Perplexity freshness problem.

04 · Method notes

How to read these numbers.

The proposed panel would preserve prompts, categories and collection windows across comparison periods. Actual sampling and scoring rules must be specified and validated before a collected benchmark is published.
